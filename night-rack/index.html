Patch: drag from a jack to another jack. Outputs sit in the inverted boxes. Any output can go into any input.
Move / unplug: grab a plug. Drop it in empty space to pull it out.
Stack cables: drag a new cable from an output that’s already in use, or Shift-drag its plug; plugs stack like real stackable cables.
Knobs: drag up/down (Shift = fine), or scroll over them. Double-click resets.
Scene: the SCENE button changes light colors and the look.
Camera: drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Double-click a module to zoom in; double-click empty space to zoom out.
Touch: one finger orbits, two fingers pan and pinch-zoom. Double-tap a module to zoom in. Tap a cable for color and length.
Controller: D-pad moves between jacks and knobs, A grabs, B lets go, Y rolls a random patch. Hold View + Menu to go back to Hedron.
